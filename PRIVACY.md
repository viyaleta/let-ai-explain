## Privacy

Let AI Explain is built so your keys and explanations stay with you. Last updated: November 2026.

**What's stored**
- Website: your API key or OpenRouter connection (only if you choose "Remember on this device"; otherwise it's forgotten when you close the tab), your history, and your settings are saved in your browser on this device. We don't have a server-side copy.
- Desktop app: your connection, history and settings are saved in a private folder on your computer.

**Where your requests go**
- What you type, and the text you ask about, is sent directly to the AI provider you connected (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, OpenRouter or ChatGPT). Their privacy policies and terms apply to those requests.
- Your keys are sent only to that provider, never to us.
- When you ask it to explain a link, the page is fetched by our server (website) or by the app (desktop) so it can be read. We don't keep those pages or log what you read.
- References are checked against Wikipedia, Crossref and the cited pages.

**Analytics**
- The website counts anonymous page views with Vercel Web Analytics. No cookies, and nothing about your explanations or keys. The desktop app has no analytics.

**Your choices**
- Clear your history anytime from History. Disconnect from the account menu to remove a saved key, and revoke keys with your provider.
